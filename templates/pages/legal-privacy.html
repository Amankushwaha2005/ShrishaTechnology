Client data remains private

We use your details only to quote, build, bill, and support your project. We do not sell your personal data.

  • What we may store: name, email, phone, messages, order IDs, and files you upload or send.
  • Login and Google sign-in data is used to keep you signed in on this website.
  • Payment card numbers are handled by the payment provider, not stored on our servers.
  • Hosting, email, or analytics tools we use may process data as needed to run the site — only for this business.
  • You can ask us to update or delete your enquiry records by contacting us. We may keep invoices as required by tax law.

Do not send Aadhaar, full card numbers, or other sensitive IDs unless we specifically ask for a KYC step.
